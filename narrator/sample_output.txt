Situation

The cleaned dataset shows total revenue of 97358.30 INR,
compared with raw revenue of 99860.20 INR. The reconciliation
difference is 2501.90 INR. Return rates are 44.4%
for COD, 14.7% for CARD, and 18.9% for UPI.

Complication

The highest-risk segment is COD in city tier 2,
with a return rate of 54.5%. The month with the highest
apparent revenue was 2026-01, at 29582.10 INR,
but after correcting quantity outliers it becomes 11637.10 INR.

Resolution

The true peak month after correcting quantity outliers is March,
with revenue of 20318.90 INR. Regional operations and finance
teams should use the cleaned revenue and return-rate findings when
evaluating performance and prioritizing return-risk actions.
